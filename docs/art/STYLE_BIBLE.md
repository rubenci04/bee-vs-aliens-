# Bee Alien — biblia visual

Fuente: instrucciones del usuario, 2026-10-10. Aplicar a cada nuevo asset.

Stylized 3D game concept art for "Bee Alien", a horizontal 2.5D arcade shooter.

## VISUAL STYLE
- cel-shaded toon look;
- 2–3 flat lighting bands;
- thin dark illustration outline;
- soft rim light;
- smooth mid-poly forms;
- handcrafted colorful game-art appearance;
- strong readable silhouettes;
- no photorealism;
- no gritty detail;
- no voxel look;
- no excessively faceted low-poly appearance.

## WORLD SCALE
Bee is tiny inside an enormous natural and human-scale world.

Nature uses:
- rich natural greens;
- ochres;
- warm yellows;
- colorful but believable natural palettes.

## ALIEN TECHNOLOGY
- dark graphite shells;
- cyan active glow;
- violet secondary energy nodes.

IMPORTANT: Cyan and violet glow belong ONLY to alien technology or alien-controlled elements.
Do not turn the whole environment neon.

## CONTROLLED FAUNA
Animals preserve their natural recognizable anatomy.

Alien control technology is:
- external;
- attached;
- removable;
- clearly foreign to the animal.

No gore. No body horror. No biological mutation.

The visual message must be: "This animal is being controlled and can be liberated."

## GAMEPLAY
Strong pure side-view silhouette because Bee Alien uses horizontal 2.5D gameplay.

## DEPTH
Use soft atmospheric depth layers and light particles such as pollen, dust, fireflies when appropriate.

## CHARACTER / CREATURE CONCEPT SHEETS
- neutral studio background;
- no environment;
- no text;
- no logos;
- no UI.

Every design should be practical to rebuild as a real mid-poly 3D model in Blender and Godot.

## Aplicación local
Usar los shaders existentes de `scripts/style/bee_toon_style.gd`. Mantener Compatibility.
Las fuentes editables van en `art_source/blender`, los modelos exportados en `assets/models`.
Trabajar en tandas pequeñas y comprobar las vistas laterales en Godot.
Las dos referencias de `art_reference/alien_technology` orientan la carcasa y los nodos;
no se incorporan como texturas ni se convierten los animales en máquinas.
